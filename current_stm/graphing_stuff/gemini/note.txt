On your Raspberry Pi, update the SERIAL_PORT variable in your logger.py script to use the USB serial node instead:

Python
SERIAL_PORT = '/dev/ttyUSB0'
(Note: Depending on how Linux enumerates the bridge chip, it might occasionally appear as /dev/ttyACM0 instead).

To confirm the exact port name on your Pi while the USB cable is plugged in, run this command in your Pi's terminal:

Bash
ls /dev/ttyUSB* /dev/ttyACM*
Whichever name appears in that list is the exact string you should put into your logger.py script.
